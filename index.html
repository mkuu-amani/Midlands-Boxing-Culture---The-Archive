The Midlands Boxing Culture Archive

Selected Works of Mkuu Amani
2017 – 2022

Mkuu Amani

Mkuu Amani is a Walsall-based photojournalist, writer, and community chronicler whose work documents the intersection of sport, grass-roots culture, and lived experience across the West Midlands.

Operating under his media imprints pmedialive, P.MEDIA.LIVE, and PML, he served as a featured photographer and contributor for Midlands Boxing Culture between 2017 and 2022. From ringside at major regional title nights—including BCB Promotions cards at Walsall Town Hall—to the canvas of local amateur clubs like Warley ABC and Pleck ABC, Amani’s lens captures both the raw intensity of the ring and the tight-knit communities behind Midlands fighters.

Alongside his photography, his journalism and cultural commentary have spotlighted underrepresented voices in the sport, including pioneering features on disability inclusion in boxing, intimate fighter profiles, and reviews of working-class British boxing cinema.
