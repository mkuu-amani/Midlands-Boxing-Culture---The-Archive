Byrone Nicholson's 'Frontline Soldiers' Out on Vimeo

Midlands Boxing Culture Archive · Mon, 20 May 2019 · Mkuu Amani

Continuing a boxing tradition that has run through his family for generations Clayton Artwell is relentless in pursuing his boxing career. Here he takes the spotlight, featuring in Byrone Nicholson's premier promo video.

Nicholson's 'Frontline Soldiers - C2B@D SOLDIERS' follows Artwell's journey from the point of his training and preparation through to his title fight against Shaun Williams at The Supreme Suite in West Bromwich.

About his video Nicholson said that it's "based on the hard work and preparation, that goes into a fighter's camp before a fighter steps into the ring for a bout."

There are some rumours of a part two. Watch this space guys...

In the meantime go ahead and check out the video.
